> 
> *Clinical research proves that **70% to 80% of diagnostic value comes directly from taking a thorough medical history** — yet under current time pressure and language barriers, vital symptoms are lost.*

Let me show you how it works in real time."*

## ❓ 3. How to Answer Tough Judge / Investor Questions

Here are the top 6 questions evaluators will ask and how to answer them with authority:

### **Q1: "Is Medikiosk attempting to diagnose patients or replace doctors?"**
> **Your Answer:**
> *"Absolutely not. Medikiosk is strictly an **intake and administrative clinical decision support tool**, not a diagnostic engine. It gathers the patient's history — which accounts for 70–80% of diagnostic data — and presents it in a standardized format so the licensed physician can make better, faster, and safer decisions."*

---

### **Q2: "How do you handle noisy hospital waiting rooms with loud background chatter?"**
> **Your Answer:**
> *"In production hardware, the kiosk uses **directional noise-cancelling microphone arrays (beamforming)** combined with close-proximity acoustic dampening. On the software side, modern Indic ASR models use spectral subtraction and voice activity detection (VAD) to filter ambient hospital noise."*

---

### **Q3: "What if a patient speaks a regional dialect or mixes Hindi with English (Hinglish)?"**
> **Your Answer:**
> *"Indic speech AI models (such as Bhashini and IndicWhisper) are trained on code-mixed speech and regional accents. Furthermore, Medikiosk provides a confirmation step after speech capture so the patient can verify or switch to typing if needed."*

---

### **Q4: "How does this integrate with the hospital's existing systems (EHR/EMR)?"**
> **Your Answer:**
> *"Medikiosk is designed around **HL7 FHIR standards** and India's **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)** architecture. The generated summaries can be directly mapped to ABHA health IDs and pushed into any standard EMR system via REST APIs."*

---

### **Q5: "What is the business model and deployment strategy?"**
> **Your Answer:**
> *"We target two primary segments:
> 1. **B2G (Public Healthcare):** Deployment in District Hospitals and Primary Health Centers (PHCs) via government health mission tenders.
> 2. **B2B (Private Hospital Chains & Clinics):** A SaaS + Kiosk Hardware lease model charged per OPD terminal per month, justified by reducing patient wait times and increasing doctor consultation capacity by 35–50%."*

---

### **Q6: "What is the primary metric of success?"**
> **Your Answer:**
> *"Our primary clinical metric is **reduction in routine intake time** (from 8–12 minutes down to under 2 minutes of doctor review) while increasing the **completeness of negative symptom and allergy documentation** to over 95%."*
